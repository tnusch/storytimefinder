Series

Every series used by an item or defined in SERIES_OVERRIDES. A series' entry sets genre/franchise/min age for all its items (whatever its type); Type only controls the public display: episodic collapses the series into one card, sequel adds "Teil N von M" badges and prev/next links, (none) shows plain cards. Saving an entry is authoritative - an unchecked genre or "(none)" removes that value. A series without an entry gets one prefilled with its items' most-used values; creating it can also remove the items' own (then ignored) copies of those values. Positions decide the episode/sequel order - fix duplicates and gaps in each series' member list.
